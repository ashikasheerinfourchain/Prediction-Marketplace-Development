## What is a Prediction Marketplace?

A prediction marketplace allows users to trade based on the potential outcomes of events using an online trading platform.

They can browse different markets and make predictions about the outcome of specific events. The platforms allow users to trade based on their predictions and use a pricing mechanism to show the likelihood of an event happening.

Some examples are Polymarket and Kalshi. Prediction markets can fall into multiple categories such as sports, political, crypto, financial, entertainment, and world events.
